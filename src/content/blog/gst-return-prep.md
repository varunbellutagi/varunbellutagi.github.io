# Preparing for GST returns without last minute stress

Consistency saves time. Build a small routine and filing becomes smooth.

## Monthly routine

- Reconcile sales invoices with GST portal data
- Check purchase invoices for missing ITC
- Keep a clean record of credit and debit notes

## Quarterly checks

- Verify vendor GSTIN status
- Review zero rated and exempt supplies
- Confirm input credit eligibility

## Annual close

Maintain a folder with returns, ledgers, and summaries for audit readiness.
