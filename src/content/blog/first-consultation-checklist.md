# First consultation checklist for new clients

Use this list to prepare for a productive first call with our team.

## What to keep ready

- Recent income documents (salary slips, invoices, or bank statements)
- Previous year tax return (if available)
- List of assets and liabilities (basic summary is enough)
- Business registrations or incorporation documents (for companies)

## Questions to consider

- What compliance deadlines are coming up?
- Which services do you need right now vs later?
- Do you need support for notices or assessments?

## Next steps

If you are ready, book a call and we will guide you on the exact documents to share.
